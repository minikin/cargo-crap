## Part 2 — Generic review (code-review skill, medium)

Eight findings, all fixed at the user's request, each pinned by a
self-test that failed first:

1. Recorded order could differ from reply order under concurrency — the
   responder now runs under the lock that records the request.
   `requests_are_recorded_in_the_order_they_were_answered` (was
   `["fast", "slow"]`).
2. `scripted` binds replies to a nondeterministic order with parallel
   clients — documented; per-request outcomes must use `respond_with`.
3–5. Shutdown relied on a wake-up connection (could steal a client's
   connection, hang forever if the connect failed, spin on accept errors) —
   the accept loop now polls a stop flag with back-off.
   `dropping_the_stub_returns_promptly` guards it.
6. No socket timeouts; connection threads never joined — 2 s read/write
   timeouts, and `Drop` joins every connection thread.
   `a_client_that_sends_nothing_is_disconnected` (hung before).
7. A chunked or unparsable-length body was recorded as empty — refused
   with a 400 naming the problem, and the input drained before closing.
   `a_chunked_request_is_refused_loudly`,
   `an_unparsable_content_length_is_refused_loudly`.
8. No custom reply headers or delayed replies — `Reply::with_header` and
   `Reply::delayed`. `a_reply_can_carry_extra_headers`,
   `a_delayed_reply_arrives_after_the_delay`.
